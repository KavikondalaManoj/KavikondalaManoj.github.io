🔧 My Approach

About

Based in Japan and supporting Honda R&D, I lead OTA and SOTA/FOTA validation programs, commission HIL test benches on dSPACE Scalexio, and write the Python automation that turns thousands of raw test cycles into decisions engineers can act on.

Along the way I've validated braking and chassis control, HVAC ECUs, exterior lighting, power tailgates, and EPS systems for OEMs including BMW, Ford, Opel, and Bentley — always with one question in mind: what happens if this fails on the road, and how do we prove in advance that it won't.
